To update, say “Refresh my Skill Atlas” in any Claude chat.

Consider disabling

Claude reads every enabled skill at the start of each chat. When two skills fit the same request, it has to guess. Turning off the ones you don't need keeps its choices clear. These are suggestions; nothing changes until you switch something off yourself.

Skills that overlap

Groups of skills that answer similar requests, with a line on when to pick each. Duplicates are better switched off; overlaps are worth knowing so you can name the right one.
